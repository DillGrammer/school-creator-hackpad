# Proposed Stardance design-stage submission

Six-key Hackpad for school, video editing and Windows gaming, with a clickable encoder and a 128×32 OLED. The two-layer 86.5×88.5 mm PCB uses a XIAO RP2040 and direct switch wiring. The OLED sits immediately to the right of the encoder, and USB-C exits at the top.

The case uses two printed shell parts, four M3 screws and heat-set inserts, plus an optional printed knob. Revision 1.1 corrects the CAD orientation and knob placement. Editable Fusion, STEP, STL, KiCad source, Gerbers, pin map, BOM and software source are included in the development archive.

Status: design and software prototype, not a physically assembled product. The case is checked against modeled component envelopes; exact supplied parts and print fit still need confirmation. Firmware input/display logic and selected helper functions have automated tests. Several app integrations remain unfinished or unverified, as listed in Software/Feature-status.md.

AI assistance: code, PCB/CAD generation and automated checks were assisted by Codex. No human work hours, physical build evidence or eligibility claims are supplied by this draft.

This draft has not been submitted. Appropriate route after readiness review: design funding/kit request, not an assembled-build reward claim. Required personal or eligibility responses must be supplied by the project owner.
